# Lockstep Language — Sprint Summary

**Sprint Goal:** Deliver a responsive front-end prototype of the Lockstep Language marketing and onboarding experience, demonstrating Tell → Show → Do and a simulated registration flow.

**Planned:** 11 story points **Completed:** 11 story points **Carryover:** 0

## Completed User Stories

| ID    | Story                     | Points | Priority | Status |
|-------|---------------------------|-------:|----------|--------|
| US-01 | Understand how it works   | 3      | High     | Done   |
| US-02 | Register for an account*  | 5      | High     | Done   |
| US-03 | Responsive display        | 3      | Medium   | Done   |

\* US-02 is delivered as an explicitly simulated, client-side-only registration flow. The form, validation, and confirmation screen all meet the sprint's acceptance criteria. No backend, account, or email is created — this is documented on the Register page and in the project README, and is scoped for a future sprint.

## Incomplete User Stories

None. All three stories in the sprint backlog met their acceptance criteria and were moved to Done.

## Deferred to Future Sprints

These were not part of this sprint and remain on the product backlog:

- Real backend, account creation, authentication, and sign-in.
- Real email verification replacing the simulated confirmation.
- Expanded vocabulary and audio library beyond the two demo words.
- Functional lesson-duration selector and real per-user progress dashboard.
- Adaptive lessons, pronunciation correction, and personalized learning progress.
- Automated unit and end-to-end tests.
