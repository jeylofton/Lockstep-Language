Competency Practice

How the four competencies are practiced

Reading

Read the word in short everyday sentences.

Listening

Listen to the word spoken and identify it.

Speaking

Repeat the word aloud and compare with the example.

Writing

Write the word and use it in your own sentence.

Pronunciation Support

Pronunciation and syllable breakdown

Each Spanish word is split into syllables and paired with audio so you can hear and repeat each part before saying the whole word. The stressed syllable is marked in bold.

About Lockstep Language

Mission Statement

Our mission is to help people learn everyday language and communicate confidently in real situations.

Why It Was Conceived

Why Lockstep Language exists

Many learners memorize vocabulary but struggle to use it in conversation. Lockstep Language was conceived to close that gap with structured instruction and repetition.

Communication over memorization

Learners move from memorizing isolated Spanish words to using real-life Spanish phrases, such as "Necesito agua."

Learner-centered teaching

Lessons follow a clear sequence and move at each learner's pace.

Accessible and flexible

Adjustable lesson lengths and readable, simple lessons make learning easy to fit into daily life.
